## 🎯 What Makes JANE Special?

JANE isn't just another chatbot. Here's what sets it apart:

### 🗣️ Four Agents, Four Voices

JANE has **four different AI agents**, each with its own personality and voice. You can switch between them depending on your mood or task:
- One for quick answers
- One for detailed research
- One for creative writing
- One for casual conversation

Each agent speaks with a distinct voice, making interactions feel natural and engaging.

### ⚡ Interruptible Conversations

Ever tried talking to a voice assistant that won't let you get a word in? JANE is different. You can **interrupt her mid-sentence** — just start talking, and she'll stop and listen to you. It feels like talking to a real person, not a machine.

### 🌐 Real Web Search

JANE can search the internet for **real, current information**. Ask about today's news, sports scores, weather, or anything else. She'll find actual results and read them to you — no made-up answers.

### 🧠 Document Memory

JANE remembers what you tell her. She can store notes, preferences, and important details. Next time you talk, she'll recall what you discussed before. It's like having an assistant with a perfect memory.

### 🔒 Local-First Privacy

Your conversations stay on **your computer**. JANE processes everything locally, so your private information never leaves your device. No cloud, no tracking, no data collection. What you say stays between you and JANE.

## 📖 How to Use JANE

### Starting Your First Conversation

1. Open JANE from your desktop or Start Menu
2. You'll see a microphone button — click it or just say **"Hey JANE"**
3. Start talking naturally, like you would to a friend
4. Watch JANE's avatar respond as she listens and speaks

### Switching Between Agents

In the bottom corner of the JANE window, you'll see four small icons. Click any one to switch agents. Each has a different color and voice so you can tell them apart.

### Asking About the Web

Just say something like:
- *"JANE, what's the weather today?"*
- *"Search for the latest tech news"*
- *"Find a good recipe for pasta"*

JANE will search the web and read the results aloud.

### Saving Memories

Tell JANE things you want her to remember:
- *"Remember that my birthday is June 15th"*
- *"Note that I prefer coffee over tea"*
- *"Save this idea for later"*

She'll store it and bring it up when relevant.

### Interrupting Mid-Sentence

If JANE is talking and you need to jump in, just start speaking. She'll pause immediately and listen to you. It's that simple.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### JANE Won't Start

- Make sure you have enough free storage space
- Restart your computer and try again
- Check that your microphone is properly connected

### Microphone Not Working

- Check your system microphone settings
- Ensure JANE has permission to use your mic (Windows will ask)
- Try a different USB port for external mics

### JANE Responds Slowly

- Close other programs to free up memory
- Make sure you're not running too many browser tabs
- Restart JANE if it's been running for hours

### Web Search Not Working

- Check your internet connection
- Try again in a few minutes — sometimes the search service is busy
